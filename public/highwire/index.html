How scoring works

Every word has a meaning fingerprint, an embedding from Amazon Titan Text Embeddings. For each pair, all 50,000 words in the dictionary are ranked by how close they are to each sign word. Your word's fit for each side comes from its rank, and its score comes from how its weaker fit ranks against every other word. The single best middle in the dictionary scores 100.

Common words are scored ahead of time, so most guesses never leave your browser. Rare words, names and two-word phrases are scored live by a small AWS Lambda function and saved, so nobody waits on the same word twice.
